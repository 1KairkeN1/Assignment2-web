Throwing Techniques

Ippon Seoi Nagi

A shoulder throw that involves placing Uke across your upper back while holding one of their arms tightly.

Tai Otoshi

A hand and body drop technique where you block the opponent's leg with your foot and drop your body weight to guide them forward and down..

O Uchi Gari

An inside trip where you step inward and reap the opponent's inner leg to break their balance backward.
